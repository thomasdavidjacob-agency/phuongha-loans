Market Update

The Portland–Vancouver market: what buyers should know right now.

By Phuong Ha · NMLS #1839449 · May 2026

Every week someone shows me a scary headline about the housing market and asks if they've missed their chance — or if they should wait for a crash. The truth is calmer and more useful than the headlines, so let's talk about what actually matters for buyers in the Portland–Vancouver metro right now.

Rates reset expectations

We're not in the 3% world anymore, and that changed the math for everyone. Higher rates mean monthly payments stretch further than they did a few years ago — which is exactly why the down-payment and program conversation matters more than ever. The buyers winning today aren't the ones who timed it perfectly; they're the ones who structured their financing smartly.

It's a tale of two pressures

Two forces are tugging against each other in our area:

  • Limited inventory — many owners locked in low rates and aren't eager to sell, which keeps supply tight and supports prices.
  • Affordability limits — higher payments cap how high prices can run before buyers step back.

The result tends to be a market that rewards preparation over speed: well-priced, move-in-ready homes still see interest, while overpriced listings sit.

Portland vs. Vancouver

One thing locals know: the river isn't just a border, it's a financial decision. Washington has no state income tax; Oregon has no statewide sales tax. That difference leads a lot of families to weigh the Vancouver side carefully. Neither is "better" — it depends on your income, spending, and lifestyle, and it's worth running the real comparison before you fall in love with a listing.

You can't control the market. You can control how ready you are to move when the right home appears.

What to do with this

Get pre-approved early, know your true budget (not just the price — the payment), and understand which programs you qualify for before you're writing an offer. In a tight market, being genuinely ready is your biggest advantage.
