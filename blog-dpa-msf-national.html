Down Payment Assistance

MSF National DPA: down payment help through the lender I work with.

By Phuong Ha · NMLS #1839449 · Updated October 2026

Most articles about down payment assistance point you to a state housing agency. Those programs are real, but they come with their own funding cycles, county-by-county limits, and approved-lender lists.

MSF National DPA is a different route. It's the down payment assistance program offered through Mortgage Solutions Financial, the lender I originate loans with. Because it's a national program, it isn't tied to one state's housing agency, and I can offer it to buyers in both Oregon and Washington.

What down payment assistance actually does

The biggest thing standing between most renters and a home isn't the monthly payment. It's the cash needed up front. Down payment assistance puts money toward that requirement so you don't have to save the whole thing yourself.

MSF National DPA is designed to sit alongside your first mortgage, so the assistance and the home loan are handled together in one process, with one loan officer.

How it compares to a state program

  • One lender, one process: the assistance comes through the same lender as your mortgage, so there's no separate agency application to chase.
  • Not limited to one state: as a national program, it isn't confined to a single state agency's rules. That matters in a metro that straddles the Oregon–Washington line.
  • Different terms: lender programs and state programs are structured differently. Neither is automatically better. The right one depends on your income, credit, and the home you're buying.

In Washington, the state housing agency's programs are still worth comparing side by side. I'll run both and show you which one leaves you better off.

The question to ask about any DPA

Down payment assistance comes in a few shapes: a grant you never repay, a forgivable loan that goes away over time, or a loan you repay when you sell or refinance. Online writing tends to call all of it a "grant," and that's often wrong.

Before you sign, ask: is this a grant, a forgivable loan, or a repayable loan? And what are the terms?

I'll walk you through exactly how the MSF National DPA assistance is structured for your loan before you commit to anything.

Who tends to be a fit

  • Buyers with income but not savings: steady earners whose biggest hurdle is the cash to close.
  • Primary residence: assistance programs are for a home you'll actually live in.
  • A workable credit profile: a minimum applies. It's usually not as high as people fear, and I can tell you where you stand.

Why the details are worth a conversation

Assistance programs change. Eligibility rules, assistance amounts, and terms all move over time, and what's true this quarter may not be true next quarter. The fastest way to know whether MSF National DPA fits you is to look at your actual numbers against the current guidelines.
