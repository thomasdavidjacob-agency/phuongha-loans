Zero & Low Down

Short on a down payment?

You may not need 20% — or even anything — down. Down Payment Assistance (DPA) programs can cover part or all of your down payment, and sometimes closing costs too, through grants or a small second loan. And true zero-down options like VA (veterans & active-duty military) and USDA (eligible rural & suburban homes) can get you into a home with little to no cash up front.
