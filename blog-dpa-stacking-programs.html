Down Payment Assistance

Can you combine DPA with FHA, VA, or conventional? Often yes — here's how.

By Phuong Ha · NMLS #1839449 · June 2026

One question I get a lot: "Can I use down payment assistance if I'm doing an FHA loan?" Or a VA loan? Or a conventional? The answer depends on the specific program, but in many cases — yes, you can layer assistance on top of your main loan type. Here's how it breaks down.

DPA + FHA: a common and powerful combination

FHA loans are one of the most common pairings with state DPA programs. WSHFC's programs allow their assistance to be used alongside FHA financing. FHA's minimum down payment can be covered by the program's second loan, reducing your cash-to-close significantly.

The full loan package looks like this: your primary FHA mortgage, with a DPA second loan (or grant) sitting behind it. You typically still make one monthly payment — the FHA loan — and the DPA has no monthly payment if it's deferred.

DPA + Conventional: possible, with program-specific rules

Some DPA programs work with conventional loans (Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac loans). Conventional loans have stricter credit requirements, but can avoid FHA mortgage insurance at higher down payment amounts. Certain programs — including some built around the HomeReady and Home Possible frameworks — are specifically designed for conventional pairings. If your credit and income qualify, a conventional + DPA combo might result in a lower total payment than FHA + DPA.

DPA + VA: less common but worth knowing

VA loans are already zero-down, so the main use of DPA alongside a VA loan is to cover closing costs. Not all DPA programs permit this combination, but some do — and for a veteran who doesn't have cash for closing costs, it can make a real difference. If you're a veteran, let's specifically look at what's available for your situation.

What determines which combination you can use

  • Which loan types the DPA program approves (most specify FHA and/or conventional)
  • Your credit score — different loan types have different minimums
  • The specific DPA program available in your county
  • The purchase price and how it aligns with program limits
The right combination isn't one-size-fits-all. It's the one that gets you the best rate, lowest payment, and least cash out of pocket for your specific situation.
